Privacy Policy for Nihongo Lens

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Nihongo Lens ("the app") is a camera-based Japanese vocabulary app. It identifies objects and dishes and shows their Japanese translation, and it can read Japanese text and translate it into English. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Nihongo Lens does not collect, store, or transmit your photos, your camera images, or the text your camera reads. Recognition and translation happen on your device. We do not have servers that receive your camera data, and we do not build a profile of you or your usage.

Camera and images

The app uses your device's camera to recognize objects, dishes, and text in real time. This processing happens on your device. In General and Dish modes, the recognition models are packaged inside the app. In Text mode, Japanese text recognition uses Google's ML Kit, delivered through Google Play services, and also runs on your device. No image, video frame, photo, or recognized text is uploaded, stored, saved to disk, or sent anywhere, including to us. Camera frames are analyzed in memory and immediately discarded.

Speech

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce Japanese words aloud. The app does not use your microphone and does not record audio.

Internet use

The app needs an internet connection for two things only: downloading language models from Google (the Japanese text-recognition model and the Japanese-to-English translation model, each downloaded once and then used on your device), and processing subscription purchases through Google Play. Nothing you point your camera at is sent over the internet.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require or create user accounts.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or location.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect any.

Subscriptions and payments

If you start a free trial or purchase a subscription, that transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.). Google processes and holds that information under its own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a subscription is active, so the app can unlock its features. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Third-party services

The app uses Google's ML Kit for on-device recognition and translation, Google Play services, and Google Play Billing for subscription payments. All three are provided by Google and operate under Google's own privacy policy linked above. No other third-party services are used.

Children's privacy

Nihongo Lens is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, consistent with the fact that we do not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
