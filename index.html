Privacy Policy for Nihongo Lens

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Nihongo Lens ("the app") is a camera-based Japanese vocabulary app that identifies objects and shows their Japanese translation. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Nihongo Lens does not collect, store, or transmit your photos or camera images. Object recognition happens entirely on your device. We do not have servers that receive your camera data, and we do not build a profile of you or your usage.

Camera and images

The app uses your device's camera to identify objects in real time. This processing happens fully on-device, using a machine learning model bundled inside the app. No image, video frame, or photo is ever uploaded, stored, saved to disk, or sent anywhere — including to us. Frames are analyzed in memory and immediately discarded.

Microphone and speech

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce Japanese words aloud. This also happens entirely on-device and does not involve sending any data off your phone.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require or create user accounts.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or location.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect any.

Subscriptions and payments

If you purchase a subscription, that transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — Google processes and holds that information under its own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a subscription is active, so the app can unlock its features.

Third-party services

The app uses Google's ML Kit for on-device object recognition and Google Play Billing for subscription payments. Both are provided by Google and operate under Google's own privacy policy linked above. Beyond standard Google Play Services functionality required to run any Android app, no other third-party services are used.

Children's privacy

Nihongo Lens is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, consistent with the fact that we do not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
